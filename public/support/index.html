Common questions

Do I need Apple Music?

Only to play music inside Manual Play. Anyone can browse, and if you use another service, Open In takes you straight to each release there.

How do I add a source?

Tap the + button to subscribe to any music blog, magazine, or writer by their website or feed address. You can also pick from suggested sources in Settings.

Why isn’t a release showing up?

Manual Play finds releases by reading what your sources write and matching it to Apple Music. If a post doesn’t name the artist and album clearly, or the record isn’t on Apple Music, it may be missed. Send me the link and I’ll take a look.

Does Manual Play sync between my devices?

Yes. Your sources and listening history sync through your iCloud account, on iPhone, Mac, Apple Watch, and CarPlay.

How do I send feedback about the beta?

In the TestFlight app, open Manual Play and tap Send Beta Feedback, or take a screenshot in Manual Play and share it with TestFlight. Email works too.
