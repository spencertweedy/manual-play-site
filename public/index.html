Manual Play makes it easy to discover great music from the writers, blogs, and magazines you already love.

Subscribe to the people whose taste you trust. Manual Play reads what they write, finds the records they’re writing about, and puts them all in one place, ready to play.

Features

  • Built on RSS

    The old web is alive, baby. Pitchfork, Stereogum, almost any music site—they all publish via RSS, and Manual Play can read their posts.

  • One tap to listen

    Manual Play magically detects the music releases your sources write about, and lets you listen to them with one click or tap.

  • Browse with any service

    Apple Music is required for in-app playback, but users of any platform can browse in Manual Play and open releases in the app they choose.

  • Read the credits, follow rabbit holes

    Why do streaming services bury this information? Manual Play puts it front-and-center. Learn about the people who created the music you love.

  • Everywhere you listen

    On iPhone, Mac, Apple Watch, and CarPlay. Your sources and listening history sync through iCloud, so you can pick up where you left off.

  • Keep it simple, stupid

    I’m absolutely tired of subscriptions. I’m tired of likes. I’m tired of “tailored” mixes. Manual Play is just a list of music, drawn from the writers you choose.
