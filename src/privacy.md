Manual Play is made by one person, Spencer Tweedy. The short version: there are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, and no tracking, in the app or on this website. I don’t collect your data, and I don’t have a server that could hold it.

## What the app keeps, and where

Manual Play remembers the sources you subscribe to and the releases you play, so it can show your feed and your listening history. That information is stored on your devices and synced between them through your own private iCloud account, using Apple’s CloudKit. I can’t see it. If you turn off iCloud for Manual Play, it stays on the device.

## Services the app talks to

To do its job, Manual Play connects directly from your device to:

- **The sources you subscribe to.** The app downloads their feeds, the same way your browser loads their websites.
- **Apple Music.** With your permission, the app uses Apple Music to match releases and play them. Apple handles that under [its own privacy policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).
- **MusicBrainz, Discogs, Wikidata, and Wikipedia.** To show credits and information about the people on a record, the app looks up release and artist names. These lookups don’t include anything about you.

Like any website, these services can see your device’s IP address when the app connects to them. When you use Open In to go to another music service, you leave Manual Play, and that service’s own privacy policy applies.

## Tips

If you leave a tip, it’s an in-app purchase handled entirely by Apple. I never see your payment details. Apple tells me that a purchase happened, but not who made it.

## This website

manualplay.com uses no cookies, no analytics, and no third-party scripts, and its fonts are served from this site rather than from Google. It’s hosted by Netlify, which, like any web host, may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security and to keep the site running.

## Children

Manual Play doesn’t collect personal information from anyone, including children.
